HOW AIDFLOW WORKS

One coordinated chain of custody.

Every proposed delivery must pass operational checks before stock is reserved.

01

Register usable aid

Capture food type, quantity, pickup window, safe-use deadline, and handling notes.

02

Check feasibility

Validate inventory, rider capacity, destination demand, resources, and travel-time buffer.

03

Track and recover

Record reservation, pickup, transit, delivery, exceptions, and operator-approved reroutes.

FAILURE IS PART OF THE PLAN

When conditions change, keep the cargo accounted for.

Expired reservations release only eligible uncollected stock. Closed shelters trigger reroute review or escalation. Every action appears in the audit trail.
